Our story

Grown by sisters,
in between the baby cuddles

~ a tiny family business from Edinburgh ~

Wee Greens is two sisters, two new babies, and a shared love of things that grow. Names of the sisters coming soon — for now, Big Sis & Wee Sis.

Our seal— pea shoot in an ink ring, est. 2026
How it all started

A windowsill,
then a shed

When we both became mums within months of each other, life suddenly got very small and very lovely: cups of tea gone cold, prams around the park, and long chats about what we wanted the next chapter to look like.

Microgreens fit. They grow fast, they taste like sunshine, and they don't need a field — just light, water, and a bit of patience between naps. Edinburgh keeps us close to the kitchens and kitchens-of-one that we love.

Grown slow. Brought quick. Tied with string.

Why microgreens

Flavour first

A handful turns porridge, eggs, soup or a sandwich into something that feels looked-after. We grow for taste, not garnish theatre.

Scottish roots

Edinburgh soil

We're not shipping chilled boxes from Perthshire. We're a local shed, cutting the morning of delivery, keeping plastic out of the tray.
